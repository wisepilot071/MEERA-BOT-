mistake we made with our niacinamide serum
- launched at 10% because thats what the market sells
- returns higher than our other products, didnt look into it for months
- went through return reasons - lots of "stinging" "redness" in first week
- pattern: mostly people who were also using a vit c or an acid
- 10% is higher than what most studies used, lot of evidence is at 2-5%
- reformulated to 5%
- returns for irritation dropped from [need real number] to [need real number]
- price same, packaging same, only concentration changed
- we should have tested on people using layered routines, not just on its own
- lesson: higher % is a marketing number not an efficacy number
