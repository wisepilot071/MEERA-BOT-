2023, visiting a supplier in Ahmedabad with a batch of hyaluronic acid
- CoA looked perfect, all specs within range
- noticed molecular weight listed as a range so wide it covered both high and low MW HA
- asked supplier, they said most buyers never ask
- high MW sits on surface and hydrates, low MW penetrates more - behave differently
- not fraud, the CoA was technically accurate, just not informative
- we now specify MW range in our purchase spec and ask for per batch data
- took us 4 extra months to find a supplier who'd commit
- not an exciting story, just paperwork. but paperwork is where formulation actually happens
- ask any brand: what MW of HA do you use and can you show batch data
