clean beauty debate - both sides get it wrong
- clean movement is right that people want to know whats in their products, and right to push for transparency
- where it breaks: "chemical free" is meaningless, water is a chemical
- "toxic" lists often based on high dose animal studies, not concentration in a cream
- parabens - well studied preservatives, alternatives sometimes less tested and more sensitising
- but the other side is also wrong when it dismisses every concern as fearmongering
- some ingredients do deserve scrutiny at certain levels
- our position: we use a preservative system chosen on data, not on marketing lists. honestly part of it is pragmatic, we want to avoid the "free from" labels debate on our packs
- reframe: question isnt natural vs synthetic, its dose, exposure, and what testing was done
